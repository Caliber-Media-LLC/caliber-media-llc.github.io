Why it exists

MADE FOR PLAYERS WHO NEED MORE THAN A KEYBOARD

Games ask for a lot of hands. Caliber gives you your voice back as a controller.

Speak, don't reach

Commands for combat, menus, navigation and squad chat. Say what you want; it presses the key. No mouse required.

Your game, your keys

Every game gets its own profile. We check the command sets against each game's real default keybinds, so "reload" means reload.

Runs on your PC

Speech recognition happens on your own computer, so it works without an account and keeps up during a match.
