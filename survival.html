Why survival players use voice

7 REASONS TO SURVIVE WITH YOUR VOICE

  1. Your hotbar, by voice

    Say quickslot one and the key is pressed. Swap to food, a torch or a weapon without taking a hand off movement.

  2. Verified for Valheim, Icarus, Rust and Minecraft

    These games have command sets checked against their real default keybinds. Every other survival game can use the general set, and you can change any key.

  3. Menus without the mouse hunt

    Teach it where a game's settings tabs are once, then say controls or graphics to jump straight there.

  4. Look it up without alt-tabbing

    PC Commands let you say a phrase to open a website — like your favorite wiki or crafting guide — launch a program, or press a key combo.

  5. Health callouts in the dark

    Show it where your health bar is once, and it calls out when you drop past 75, 50 and 25 percent — so you know before it's too late.

  6. Built for long sessions and co-op

    Use push-to-talk so chatting with your crew doesn't fire commands, or say mute to pause it and unmute to carry on.

  7. Company for the long haul

    Vikings who curse in Norse, dwarves who curse in Dwarven, an 1849 gold miner full of tall tales. Pick a character that fits your world.

Straight talk

WHAT TO EXPECT

  • One spoken command presses one key, the same way your keyboard does. It doesn't play the game for you.
  • Speech recognition is English only, on Windows 10 and 11. A noisy room or a poor microphone can make it mishear you — push-to-talk helps.
  • Some online games with strict anti-cheat may not allow programs that send keys. Check a game's rules, especially on official servers.
  • Some voices are rated R for adult language. Every pack shows its rating, so you know before you pick.
  • The characters are Caliber Media originals. The voices are AI-generated with ElevenLabs from our own scripts.
  • It's a beta. Tell us in the Discord which survival game you want next.
