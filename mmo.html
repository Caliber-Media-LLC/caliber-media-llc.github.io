Why MMO players use voice

7 REASONS TO RAID WITH YOUR VOICE

  1. Your action bars, by voice

    Say quickslot one and the key is pressed. Put the abilities you reach for least on words, and keep your hands on movement.

  2. Verified command sets for WoW and FFXIV

    World of Warcraft and Final Fantasy XIV have command sets checked against each game's real default keybinds. Moved your keys? Change any binding to match.

  3. A profile for every character's game

    Each game gets its own profile, and CaliberEngine can switch to it when it sees the game start. Nothing to click before you log in.

  4. Menus without the mouse hunt

    Teach it where a game's settings tabs are once, then say controls or graphics to jump straight there.

  5. Health callouts during the pull

    Show it where your health bar is once, and it calls out when you drop past 75, 50 and 25 percent — so your eyes can stay on the boss.

  6. One word for long commands

    Add your own abbreviations and quick phrases, so a short word stands in for a longer command — and so names it mishears can be fixed your way.

  7. A companion from your race

    An Orc shaman who calls you young one. A Gnome warlock who treats demons like lab equipment. 24 race-themed characters, each cursing in their own tongue.

Straight talk

WHAT TO EXPECT

  • One spoken command presses one key, the same way your keyboard does. It doesn't play the game for you or run rotations on its own.
  • Speech recognition is English only, on Windows 10 and 11. Fantasy names can be tricky for it — add your own abbreviations for those.
  • Check each game's rules for third-party programs. We can't promise every game accepts programs that send keys.
  • The characters are Caliber Media originals inspired by the races players love. They aren't made or endorsed by Blizzard Entertainment. The voices are AI-generated with ElevenLabs from our own scripts.
  • It's a beta. Tell us in the Discord what your class needs.
